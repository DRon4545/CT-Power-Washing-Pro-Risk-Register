# CT Power Washing Pro --- Risk Register

## Overview

This repository contains a practical risk-management register for CT
Power Washing Pro, a power-washing/exterior-cleaning business assumed to
generate approximately \$250,000 in annual profit.

The project identifies key operational, safety, financial,
environmental, reputational, and business-continuity risks and assigns
each risk an impact score, probability score, priority level, mitigation
actions, owner, and target due date.

## Repository Contents

-   `risk-register.md` --- Complete risk register with 20 identified
    risks.
-   `risk-matrix.md` --- 5×5 impact/probability matrix and scoring
    methodology.
-   `NOTES.md` --- Development notes, assumptions, reasoning, actions
    taken, and recommended next steps.

## Risk Scoring

Risk Score = **Impact × Probability**

### Impact

1.  Insignificant
2.  Minor
3.  Moderate
4.  Major
5.  Severe/Catastrophic

### Probability

1.  Rare
2.  Unlikely
3.  Possible
4.  Likely
5.  Almost Certain

### Priority

     Score Priority   Status
  -------- ---------- -----------
      1--4 Low        🟢 Green
      5--9 Moderate   🟡 Yellow
    10--15 High       🟠 Orange
    16--25 Critical   🔴 Red

## Important Disclaimer

The initial scores are management estimates because detailed
company-specific data was not provided. The register should be validated
against actual insurance policies, claims, incidents, financial results,
equipment records, staffing information, customer complaints, and
applicable regulatory requirements.

## Review Cadence

-   Critical risks: review monthly.
-   High risks: review quarterly.
-   Moderate risks: review semiannually.
-   Update the register after major incidents, claims, regulatory
    changes, new services, or significant operational changes.
