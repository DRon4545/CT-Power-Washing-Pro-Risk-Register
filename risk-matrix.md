# CT Power Washing Pro --- 5×5 Risk Matrix

## Scoring Method

**Risk Score = Impact × Probability**

### Impact Scale

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
                         Score Impact Level          Description
  ---------------------------- --------------------- ---------------------
                             1 Insignificant         Minimal operational
                                                     or financial effect

                             2 Minor                 Limited disruption;
                                                     manageable cost

                             3 Moderate              Noticeable
                                                     disruption, cost, or
                                                     customer impact

                             4 Major                 Significant
                                                     financial,
                                                     operational, legal,
                                                     safety, or
                                                     reputational effect

                             5 Severe/Catastrophic   Potential for major
                                                     injury, substantial
                                                     loss, prolonged
                                                     interruption, or
                                                     serious liability
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Probability Scale

    Score Probability Level   Description
  ------- ------------------- ----------------------------------------------
        1 Rare                Unlikely to occur under normal conditions
        2 Unlikely            Could occur, but not expected frequently
        3 Possible            Reasonably possible during normal operations
        4 Likely              Expected to occur periodically
        5 Almost Certain      Expected to occur frequently or repeatedly

## How to Use the Matrix

1.  Assign an Impact score from 1--5.
2.  Assign a Probability score from 1--5.
3.  Multiply the two scores.
4.  Use the resulting score to determine the priority level.
5.  Assign an owner and mitigation action.
6.  Set a target completion date.
7.  Reassess the score after mitigation.

## Review Guidance

-   **Critical:** Immediate management attention; review monthly.
-   **High:** Active mitigation required; review quarterly.
-   **Moderate:** Monitor and improve controls; review semiannually.
-   **Low:** Monitor as part of normal management.

The matrix is a management tool. Scores should be recalibrated as actual
company data becomes available.
